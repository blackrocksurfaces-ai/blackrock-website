Why do Calgary garages benefit from a sealed floor?

Calgary winters bring road salt, slush and repeated freeze-thaw cycles, especially when a chinook rolls through. Bare concrete is porous: salty meltwater soaks in, stains the slab and, over time, can flake and pit the surface. Bare concrete also sheds dust that gets tracked into the house.

A sealed epoxy floor is seamless and non-porous, so salt, oil and slush sit on top and wipe or squeegee off instead of soaking in.

Can an epoxy floor be installed in winter?

Yes. We work year-round, including winter. The space needs to be kept heated to the temperature the coating needs during install and cure. Attached and heated garages are usually fine; we'll confirm at your quote.

Why does surface prep matter so much?

Most coating failures, like peeling or lifting, come down to poor prep. We diamond-grind the concrete to open up the surface so the coating bonds into it, then repair cracks, spalls and pits before any coating goes down. That step is what separates a professional floor from a roll-on kit from a big-box store.

How long does the install take, and when can I use the floor?

Most residential garages take 1 to 2 days, depending on size, the slab's condition and the system. Light foot traffic is usually fine within about a day; vehicles need longer for the coating to fully cure. We'll give you exact times for your floor and the temperature on the day.

What happens to the cracks in my concrete?

Cracks and spalls are repaired before coating. Coatings bridge hairline cracks well, but a slab that is still moving can show cracks through over time. If we see signs of that, we'll tell you at the quote.

How do I take care of an epoxy floor?

Sweep or dust-mop regularly and mop with a mild cleaner. Avoid soap-heavy or acidic cleaners, which leave a film. In winter, squeegee or rinse off salty slush now and then so it doesn't sit on the floor, and put a mat where you step out of the vehicle.
