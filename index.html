Why epoxy

Bare concrete stains, dusts and cracks. A sealed floor doesn't.

  • Seamless and sealed. No grout lines or pores for oil, salt and water to soak into.
  • Chemical and stain resistant. Road salt, fuel and oil wipe up instead of etching in.
  • Easy to clean. Sweep, mop, done. No more concrete dust tracked into the house.
  • Brighter space. A gloss finish reflects light and makes the room feel bigger.
