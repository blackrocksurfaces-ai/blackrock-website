Floor systems

Three systems. One standard of work.

Flake epoxy

Decorative vinyl flakes broadcast into an epoxy base, then locked in with a clear topcoat. It's the go-to system for garages because it handles Calgary winters: salt, slush, studded tires and dripping trucks.

  • Residential garages
  • Basements and utility rooms
  • Workshops and storage areas
  • Mudrooms and laundry rooms

Metallic epoxy

Quartz broadcast

Coloured quartz aggregate is broadcast into epoxy in layers, creating a dense floor with built-in grip. It's the system for spaces with heavy traffic, frequent washing or safety requirements.

Why epoxy

Bare concrete stains, dusts and cracks. A sealed floor doesn't.

  • Seamless and sealed. No grout lines or pores for oil, salt and water to soak into.
  • Chemical and stain resistant. Road salt, fuel and oil wipe up instead of etching in.
  • Easy to clean. Sweep, mop, done. No more concrete dust tracked into the house.
  • Brighter space. A gloss finish reflects light and makes the room feel bigger.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

How long does an install take?

Most residential garages take 1 to 2 days, depending on the size, the slab's condition and the system. We'll give you a timeline with your quote.

When can I walk or park on it?

Light foot traffic is usually fine within about a day. Vehicles need longer for the coating to fully cure. We'll give you exact times for your system and the temperature on the day.

Can you coat a floor in winter?

Yes, as long as the space can be kept heated to the temperature the coating needs during install and cure. Attached and heated garages are usually fine.

What about cracks in my concrete?

Cracks and spalls are repaired before coating. Coatings bridge hairline cracks well, but a slab that's still moving can telegraph cracks through over time. We'll flag that at the quote.

Is it slippery?

A plain gloss coat can be slick when wet. Flake and quartz systems add texture, and we can add anti-slip additive to the topcoat.

How do I clean it?

Sweep or dust-mop regularly, and mop with a mild cleaner. Avoid soap-heavy or acidic cleaners that leave a film.

How much does it cost?

It depends on square footage, slab condition and system. Every quote is free and done on-site so the price is accurate, not a guess.
